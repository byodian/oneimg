# Migrate with a strangler and renderer-first risk spike

OneIMG will incrementally replace the current distributed state and export paths behind deep modules, temporary routes, and feature flags, beginning with an isolated Canonical Renderer and Local SVG Adapter risk spike. Legacy OneIMG and longpic remain recovery and fixture references until semantic migration and conformance pass. This accepts temporary coexistence in exchange for evidence before UI rewrite, non-destructive data rollout, small reversible changes, and no big-bang default-route switch.
