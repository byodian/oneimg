# Keep Smart Paste local and deterministic

Smart Paste will parse supported clipboard content locally through deterministic rules, preserve the user's wording and meaningful order, discard unsafe or foreign presentation styles, and never require an AI or network request. Ambiguous restructuring or rewriting belongs to a separately invoked AI Assist that previews its proposal and requires distinct authorization before content leaves the device. This sacrifices invisible “magic” in exchange for immediate response, offline use, predictable undo, privacy, and user trust.
